Delete Your Account

Unsift — developed by Vakbridge

You can request deletion of your Unsift account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account — email address, display name, and authentication credentials
  • Cloud-stored card metadata — merchant names, balances, expiry dates, and dates added
  • Anonymous training data (if you opted in) — anonymized scan data already submitted cannot be linked back to you and is retained for model improvement

On-device data (card numbers, PINs, barcodes) is stored exclusively in your device's secure hardware and is automatically removed when you uninstall the app.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — In the App

Open Unsift → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and all cloud data will be permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By Email

Send a deletion request to info@vakbridge.com from the email address associated with your account. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by reply.
