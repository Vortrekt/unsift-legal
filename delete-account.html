Delete Your Account

Unsift — developed by VakBridge Inc.

You can delete your Unsift account and its associated data at any time. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, and sign-in credentials
  • Your gift cards: merchant names, balances, expiry dates, and the encrypted card numbers, PINs, and codes stored in our cloud database
  • Related records: balance history, scan records, and notification preferences and history
  • On this device: your app PIN, scheduled reminders, and saved nearby-store information

What Is Kept

  • Anonymous training data (only if you opted in to "Help Us Improve"): it is not linked to your account, so it cannot be identified as yours or deleted
  • Feedback you sent us by email: kept for up to 12 months
  • Crash reports: deleted automatically after 90 days

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1 — In the App

Open Unsift → Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and all cloud data are deleted immediately. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Option 2 — By Email

Send a deletion request to info@vakbridge.com from the email address associated with your account. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by reply.
