Privacy Policy

Effective Date: RELEASE_DATE

Unsift ("we," "our," or "the app") is a gift card scanning and management app developed by VakBridge Inc. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. Unsift is currently offered in the United States.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name and profile photo URL (if your sign-in provider supplies them)
  • Sign-in method (Email, Google, or Apple) and when you last signed in

Gift Card Details

For each card you save, the following is stored in our cloud database so your cards are available when you sign in:

  • Merchant name (e.g., "Amazon," "Starbucks")
  • Balance, and a history of balance changes you make
  • Expiration date and card status (e.g., unused or used)
  • Barcode format type and the date the card was added

Card Numbers, PINs, and Codes (Encrypted)

Card numbers, PINs, claim codes, access codes, and barcode values are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before they are saved to our cloud database. The encryption key is unique to your account; it is generated by our server and delivered only to your signed-in device. We never store these details in readable form. Because the key is generated on our server rather than only on your device, this is not end-to-end encryption.

Card Photos

When you scan a gift card, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection, through our server, to Google's Gemini API, which reads the fields printed on the card. This happens on every scan and is required for the app to read your card. We do not store your card photo in our database or file storage, and the copy on your device is deleted once the scan is finished.

Under Google's terms for paid API use, Google does not use these photos to improve its products. Google keeps them for a limited period solely to detect and prevent misuse of its service, and they may be processed in any country where Google operates.

Text recognition and barcode reading on your device are done by Google ML Kit, entirely on your phone.

Location (Optional)

If you turn on nearby-store reminders and allow location access, Unsift uses your device's precise location to find stores near you where your gift cards can be used. If you also allow "Always" access, the app can remind you about a card when you arrive at one of those stores, even when the app is closed.

  • Your location and the merchant names on your cards are sent to our server to look up nearby stores. Our server does not store or log your location.
  • To look up store addresses, our server sends only the merchant name and an approximate area (about 5 km across) to OpenStreetMap. No information that identifies you is included.
  • The list of nearby stores and the location it was looked up from are saved on your device so the app can tell when you arrive at a store. Matching stores to your cards happens on your device.

You can turn location off at any time in the app's notification settings or in your phone's settings. The rest of the app works without it.

Notifications

Reminders about expiring cards, unused balances, and nearby stores are created on your device. To avoid sending too many reminders, we store your notification preferences, the merchants you have muted, and a record of when reminders were scheduled and opened. Reminder text can include a merchant name and card balance.

Feedback (Optional)

If you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, category, device platform and OS version, and your account email address, delivered to us by email through EmailJS. If you attach a screenshot, it is uploaded to our file storage and a link to it is included in the email. Please do not attach screenshots that show card numbers or PINs.

Anonymous Training Data (Optional)

If you opt in to "Help Us Improve," we collect scan data to improve card recognition. This data is not linked to your account and includes:

  • Text read from the card, with card numbers, PINs, and codes removed on your device before it is sent
  • Detected and confirmed merchant names
  • Which fields were detected (without their values)
  • Extraction quality records: when we test a new extraction method, we record whether the methods agreed on each field. These records contain merchant names and agreement results only, never card numbers, PINs, or codes

We do not collect card photos for training.

Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports. They include your device model, OS version, a crash stack trace, the steps the app was taking when it crashed, and your account ID, so we can investigate problems you report. They do not include card numbers, PINs, codes, or photos.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service: Store your gift cards and sync them to your signed-in devices
  • Read your cards: Extract the merchant, card number, and codes from your card photo so you don't have to type them
  • Remind you: Send reminders about expiring or unused cards and, if enabled, nearby stores
  • Show merchant branding: Look up a merchant's logo and colors using the merchant name
  • Authenticate you: Verify your identity to protect your data
  • Fix bugs: Use crash reports to find and resolve problems
  • Improve card recognition: Use anonymous training data, if you opt in

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How We Protect Your Information

  • Card numbers, PINs, and codes are encrypted on your device before being saved, as described above
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest on Google Cloud
  • App lock: an optional PIN and Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint protect access to your card details on your device
  • Access rules ensure each account can only read its own data
  • Secret credentials for the paid services we use are kept on our server and never shipped in the app

4. Service Providers

We use the following providers to operate Unsift. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Crashlytics): account sign-in, data storage, our server, and crash reporting (Firebase Privacy)
  • Google Gemini API: reading fields from card photos
  • Google ML Kit: on-device text and barcode recognition; nothing is sent to Google for this step
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): store locations, given only a merchant name and an approximate area
  • Brandfetch: merchant logos and colors, given only a merchant name
  • EmailJS: delivering feedback you choose to send
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: optional sign-in methods

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the app, or when required by law.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correct: You can view and edit all of your saved card information in the app
  • Delete a card: Deleting a card removes it, including its encrypted details, from our database and your device
  • Delete your account: In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This immediately deletes your account and all associated data from our servers. You can also request deletion by email
  • Location and notifications: You can turn these off at any time in the app or your phone's settings
  • Training data: You can opt out at any time in Settings > Privacy & Data

Depending on the state you live in, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. You can exercise them using the options above or by emailing us; we will respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for doing so.

7. Data Retention

  • Account and card data (including encrypted card details, balance history, scan records, and notification records): kept until you delete the card or your account
  • Card photos: not stored by us; kept by Google for a limited period for abuse prevention, as described above
  • Location: not stored on our servers; the nearby-store information on your device is removed when you sign out, delete your account, or turn off nearby reminders
  • Feedback: emails and screenshots are kept for up to 12 months
  • Crash reports: kept for 90 days
  • Anonymous training data: kept indefinitely; because it is not linked to your account, it cannot be found or deleted for a specific user

8. Children's Privacy

Unsift is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page, and for significant changes we may also notify you in the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
